Welcome! I am a mathematical biologist working broadly in the areas of evolution and ecology. I develop mathematical models to understand the biological processes that underpin adaptive evolution. Stochastic processes, ODEs, PDEs and computational methods are all important in my research.


Prospective students: I am looking for graduate students with a strong record in applied mathematics or similar fields. Please see [here](https://www.math.uwo.ca/graduate/prospective_graduate_students.html "here") for more information about the graduate program in the Department of Mathematics at Western University.
